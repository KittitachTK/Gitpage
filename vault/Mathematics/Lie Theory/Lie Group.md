A **Lie group** is a group that is also a smooth manifold, with smooth multiplication and inversion. Examples: $U(1)$, $SU(2)$, $SU(3)$.

Its tangent space at the identity is a Lie algebra $\mathfrak g$ with bracket $[X,Y]$; see [[Linear Transformations]] for the matrix picture.
