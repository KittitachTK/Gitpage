The **mass gap** is the statement that the lowest excitation above the vacuum has strictly positive energy.

## Statement

> [!conjecture] Yang–Mills existence and mass gap
> For any compact simple gauge group $G$, a non-trivial quantum Yang–Mills theory exists on $\mathbb R^4$ and has a mass gap $\Delta > 0$ [@JaffeWitten2000].

## Relation to the Spectral Gap

On the lattice, the mass gap is the [[Spectral Gap]] of the transfer matrix in the infinite-volume limit:

$$
\Delta = E_1 - E_0 > 0 .
$$

It is equivalent to [[Exponential Clustering]] of correlations. ==This equivalence is the key bridge.==
