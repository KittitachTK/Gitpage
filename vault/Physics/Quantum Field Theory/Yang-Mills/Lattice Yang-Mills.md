Wilson's lattice formulation [@Wilson1974] replaces $A_\mu$ by link variables $U_{x,\mu}\in G$ with action

$$
S(U) = \beta \sum_{p} \Big(1 - \tfrac{1}{N}\operatorname{Re}\operatorname{tr} U_p\Big).
$$
